WristCommand LTE 개인정보 안내

시행 2026-10-02 · 변경 이력은 맨 아래

WristCommand LTE(시계 앱)는 테슬라 주인이 가민 시계로 자기 차를 잠그고 열고 조종하는 Connect IQ 앱으로, 인터넷 명령에 사용자 본인의 개발자 계정을 씁니다. 함께 쓰는 시계 화면은 WristCommand Face입니다. 개인 개발자가 무료로 운영하며, 테슬라(Tesla, Inc.)·가민과 관계가 없습니다.

1. 개발자가 보관하는 정보

앱이 쓰는 정보는 개발자가 보관하지 않습니다. 개발자는 사용자 정보를 모아 두는 서버·데이터베이스를 두지 않고, 앱에 분석 도구도 넣지 않았습니다. 이 판은 로그인 중계 서버도 쓰지 않습니다.

다만 문의 메일을 보내면 그 메일(보낸 주소·내용)은 답변에만 쓰고 답변을 마친 날부터 1년 뒤 지웁니다. 메일은 Google(Gmail, 미국) 메일함에 보관됩니다. 이 처리는 문의에 답하기 위한 것으로 동의를 따로 받지 않습니다(개인정보 보호법 제15조 제1항 제4호, 2026-09-11 시행본).

2. 처리 목적

사용자 본인의 개발자 계정으로 테슬라 계정 연결, 차에 명령 보내기, 차 상태를 시계에 보여 주기.

3. 처리하는 정보와 저장 위치

  • 내 개발자 계정 값(본인 개발자 앱의 Client ID·Client Secret·웹 주소) — 시계 안. 사용자가 준비 페이지에서 한 줄로 묶어, 시계가 띄운 도우미 페이지에 붙여넣습니다. 이 한 줄은 인터넷으로 보내지 않고 폰의 Garmin Connect 앱이 받아 시계로 넘깁니다. 시계는 이 값으로 테슬라에 웹 주소를 등록하고, 로그인 코드를 테슬라와 직접 토큰으로 바꿉니다
  • 테슬라 로그인 토큰(접근·갱신) — 시계 안
  • 차 이름·차대번호(VIN) — 시계 안. 명령을 보낼 차를 가리키는 데 씁니다
  • 차 상태(잠금·배터리·주행 가능 거리·실내 온도) — 시계 안, 화면 표시용
  • 시계 키 — 시계 안에서 만들어지는 서명용 키로, 사용자가 키카드로 차에 등록합니다
  • 준비·도우미 페이지를 열 때와 준비 페이지에서 [게시 확인]을 누를 때의 접속 정보 — GitHub(6장)

4. 보유 기간과 파기

시계 안의 정보는 앱을 지우면 함께 지워집니다. 내 개발자 계정 값과 로그인은 시계의 「내 개발자 계정 지우기」로 바로 지울 수 있습니다(설정하다 실패하면 그 값은 곧바로 지워집니다). 테슬라 계정에서 본인 개발자 앱의 권한을 거두면 시계에 남은 토큰도 쓸 수 없게 됩니다.

5. 동의와 거부

시계 앱은 「내 개발자 계정」 연결이나 로그인 전에 위 내용을 보여 주고 동의를 받습니다. 동의하지 않을 수 있으며, 그러면 내 개발자 계정 연결과 인터넷 명령은 쓸 수 없고 차 가까이에서 블루투스로만 쓸 수 있습니다. 동의 문구의 목적·항목·보유 기간이 바뀌면 시계에서 다시 동의를 받습니다.

WristCommand LTE Privacy

Effective 2026-10-02 · change history at the bottom

WristCommand LTE (watch app) is a Connect IQ app for Tesla owners to lock, unlock and control their own car from a Garmin watch, using their own developer account for internet commands. The watch face used with it is WristCommand Face. It is run free of charge by an individual developer and is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc. or Garmin.

1. What the developer keeps

The developer keeps none of the data the app uses. The developer runs no server or database that collects user data, and the app contains no analytics. This edition also uses no sign-in relay server.

If you email us, however, that email (sender address and content) is used only to reply and is deleted one year after the reply. Emails are kept in a Google (Gmail, USA) mailbox. This is done to answer your request, without separate consent (Article 15(1)(4) of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, as in force from 2026-09-11).

2. Purpose

Connecting your Tesla account through your own developer account, sending commands to your car, and showing its status on the watch.

3. Data processed and where it stays

  • Your developer account values (your own developer app's Client ID, Client Secret and web address) — on the watch. You bundle them into one line on the setup page and paste it into the helper page the watch opens. The line is not sent over the internet: the Garmin Connect app on your phone receives it and passes it to the watch. The watch uses the values to register your web address with Tesla and exchanges the sign-in code directly with Tesla
  • Tesla sign-in tokens (access and refresh) — on the watch
  • Car names and VINs — on the watch, used to address your car
  • Car status (lock, battery, range, cabin temperature) — on the watch, for display
  • Watch key — a signing key created on the watch that you pair to your car with its key card
  • Connection data when you open the setup or helper page, or tap [Check] on the setup page — GitHub (section 6)

4. Retention and deletion

Data on the watch is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your developer account values and sign-in can be removed at once with "Remove my developer account" on the watch (values from a failed setup are removed right away). Revoking your own developer app's access in your Tesla account makes any tokens left on the watch unusable.

5. Consent and declining

The watch app shows this information and asks for consent before connecting "My developer account" or signing in. You may decline; then your developer account cannot be connected, internet commands are unavailable and the app works over Bluetooth near the car only. If the purpose, items or retention period in the consent text change, the watch asks for consent again.

Your own GitHub site (username.github.io) that holds your public key file runs under your own account, under the terms and privacy statement between you and GitHub. That site and the key file are public pages anyone can see; the developer does not receive or manage them.

Commands, status requests and the web address registration are sent by the watch, with your own Tesla account and your own developer app, through the Garmin Connect app on your phone directly to Tesla's servers (Tesla, Inc., USA). What goes to Tesla is your developer account values (Client ID, Client Secret, web address), the sign-in code and tokens, VINs and car commands, each time you send a command, request or registration, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Because none of it passes through the developer, this is not a provision to a third party or an international transfer by the developer. Processing and retention at Tesla follow the Tesla Customer Privacy Notice, and the part through your phone follows Garmin's privacy policy. When your phone is unavailable, commands sent directly from the watch to the car over Bluetooth do not pass through any server.

7. Security

  • All internet traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Your developer account secret stays in the watch app's storage only, never in Garmin Connect settings, on screen or in logs.
  • The helper page is a static page that blocks all network requests. The setup page blocks them too, except that tapping [Check] in card 7 reads your own public key address (username.github.io) once; nothing you typed is sent. The one line contains your secret, so the page asks you to clear your clipboard (including any keyboard clipboard history) afterwards.
  • Tokens stay in the watch app's storage and are never shown on screen or written to logs.

8. Location

The app does not request Tesla's vehicle location permission and never reads your car's location. The watch face computes sunrise and sunset on the watch from the observation point of the watch's weather feature; the watch face has no communication permission and never sends this out.

9. Watch capabilities used

Bluetooth (talks to the car directly) · Communications (reaches Tesla's servers through your phone) · Complications (shows car status on the watch face) · Background (declared but currently unused). The watch face only reads complications.

10. Cookies and analytics

None.

11. Children

Not for children under 14.

12. Sharing

We do not sell or share user information.

13. Your rights

The developer keeps no app records, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side; emails you sent us can be accessed or deleted on request. If you disagree with our reply, you can ask again at the same address. Data on the watch is deleted when you uninstall the app; to withdraw consent, uninstall the app and revoke your own developer app's access in your Tesla account. Questions are welcome at the address below.

Shown on the watch before connecting or signing in when you tap "My developer account" or "LTE sign-in". The version and time you tapped "Agree" are recorded only on your watch.

  • To connect your Tesla account, send commands to your car and show its status, the app uses the following.
  • Data used: your own developer app's Client ID, Client Secret and web address, Tesla sign-in tokens, car names and VINs, car status (lock, battery, range, cabin temperature).
  • This data is stored only on this watch and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is not stored on any developer server.
  • You bundle your developer app values into one line on the setup page and paste it into the helper page the watch opens. The line is not sent over the internet: the Garmin Connect app on your phone receives it and passes it to the watch. The helper page is loaded from GitHub Pages (GitHub, a US company), which may process connection data such as your IP address. The watch uses the values to register your web address with Tesla and exchanges the sign-in code directly with Tesla, without any relay server. Remove them any time with Remove my developer account.
  • Commands and status requests go through Tesla's servers (US) under Tesla's Privacy Notice: tesla.com/legal/privacy
  • You may decline. Then your developer account cannot be connected, internet commands are unavailable and the app works over Bluetooth only. To decline, press Back.
  • Privacy officer: Kim Hongjeong, the WristCommand developer (kimsalgi21@gmail.com). You may ask to access, correct, delete or suspend processing of your personal data, and you can withdraw consent at any time free of charge by uninstalling the app and revoking your own developer app's access in your Tesla account. This consent text is always available on the Privacy page.
  • Details: wristcommand-key.github.io/own/privacy
  • Tapping Agree means you consent to the app using the data above on your watch, and acknowledge that GitHub and Tesla process data under their own privacy policies and that you have received Tesla's Privacy Notice.
